Flagged — Pi standoffs

The rear pair of Orange Pi standoffs sits at x = −68, and the deck ends at −63. They are not attached to anything: chassis_top() returns 3 disjoint solids with a bounding box reaching x = −72.8.

build() misses it — a compound of three valid solids is valid and has positive volume — and the Pi's rear holes have nothing to bolt to. OPI_HOLES = 92 × 54 about OPI_X = −22 needs OPI_X ≥ −17 to land on the deck.

Flagged — BMS bay

The front bay's clear length in X is 60 − 42.1 = 17.9 mm, between the pack's end stop and the front wall. BMS_L is 64.

The retaining rib is 30 mm wide in Y and 13 tall, which matches BMS_W = 27 and BMS_H = 13 — so only the 64 has nowhere to go. The outline is already on README.md's verify list; measure a real board before moving anything.

The three tight fits

IMU 0.8 mm. Pack top at z = 21.4, deck underside at 25, board plus components 2.8. imu_clear() prints what is left every build, because the pack is a payload and interference() cannot see one.

Camera slot ~1 mm. Floor 15.36 (the roll cradles), back x = 63, front x = 68.1 (the fork's rear arm sweep), ceiling the pedestal's flattened base.

Deck bosses vs. cells. The mid screws at |y| = 41 stand beside the pack; their nut channels open along +X because inboard is the battery.

Order it goes together

Deck nuts → XT shells from inside → pack and BMS → pedestal bolted to the deck from underneath, deck still off → IMU up under its tabs → deck onto the tray → Pi → GPS mast → camera → L2.

Half of these nuts stop being reachable one step later, which is what fixes the order rather than convenience.
